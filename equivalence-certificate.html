RK education Group Equivalence Certificate

An Equivalency Certificate is an official document that confirms your educational qualification from another country is equal to the standards set by the UAE’s Ministry of Education. It is a mandatory requirement if you plan to pursue higher education, apply for certain jobs or get your professional license approved in the UAE. Simply put, it validates that your degree or diploma is recognized and accepted locally.

At RK Education, we make the equivalency certificate process simple and stress-free. From document preparation to submission and follow-up, our team ensures that your qualifications are evaluated and approved without unnecessary delays. With our guidance, you can confidently move forward with your academic or career goals in the UAE, knowing your education is officially recognized.

RK education Group Documents Required

To complete the attestation or equivalency process in the UAE, you will need to submit key documents such as your original degree or diploma certificate, final mark sheets or transcripts, passport copy, visa copy and any official letters from the university or board if applicable. Additional documents may be requested depending on your qualification and the country of issue, but our team at RK Group will guide you through every step to ensure nothing is missed
